# Glossary

- **Base Capture**: A high-quality HEIC image captured natively without computational enhancements (e.g., Smart HDR or Deep Fusion). It relies on the 10-bit color depth of the HEIC format to serve as a pure foundation for later editing, contrasting with standard iPhone photos that are heavily processed.
- **Primary Wide**: The device's main wide-angle physical lens (typically ~26mm equivalent). This is the only lens the application supports to prevent computational lens switching and digital zoom degradation.
- **Shutter Ring**: The visual feedback mechanism for capturing a photo. Instead of a screen flash, a thin white ring originates from the shutter button and rapidly expands across the screen.
